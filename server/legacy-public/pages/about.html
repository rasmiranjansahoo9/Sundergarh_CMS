Our Story

About Sundergarh

Sundergarh carries a distinctive character where forests, wildlife, water systems, hills and human settlements meet. The Forest & Wildlife Division works to protect this natural heritage while encouraging responsible development and meaningful participation of local communities.

The division's work connects everyday protection with long-term conservation — from forest management and wildlife protection to awareness, habitat care, fire prevention and citizen engagement.

Nature as a Legacy

Every forest path, stream and hillside forms part of a larger ecological story — one that belongs to both the present generation and those yet to come.

History of Sundergarh

From its historical roots to its present-day identity, Sundergarh has evolved as a district where cultural heritage and natural landscapes remain deeply connected.

Before 1948

The region was associated with the former Gangpur and Bonai princely states, carrying a rich history of people, forests and traditional landscapes.

Our Responsibility

Protecting Nature. Serving People.

The Forest & Wildlife Division brings together protection, conservation and public service. Its role is not only to safeguard natural resources, but also to build awareness and partnerships that make conservation stronger on the ground.

ProtectionForest and wildlife protection, patrolling and enforcement.
RestorationRegeneration, habitat improvement and landscape care.
ParticipationCommunity awareness and collaborative conservation.
Responsible TourismNature experiences that respect ecological limits.
