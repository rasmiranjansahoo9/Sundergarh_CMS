FLAGSHIP SPECIES

Asian Elephant

Elephas maximus

A highly social herbivore whose movements connect forest habitats and influence vegetation structure.

HabitatForest, grassland & water sources
DietGrasses, leaves, bark & fruits
FocusHuman-wildlife coexistence
Habitat corridors and safe movement routes are important for elephant conservation.

CARNIVORE

Leopard

Panthera pardus

An adaptable carnivore that contributes to maintaining ecological balance through its role as a predator.

HabitatForest & rocky landscapes
DietCarnivorous
Secure habitat and adequate prey availability support leopard conservation.

FOREST DWELLER

Sloth Bear

Melursus ursinus

A forest-dependent mammal associated with diverse woodland habitats and a varied diet.

HabitatDry & moist forest
DietFruits, insects & plant matter
Protection of forest habitat and reduction of disturbance benefit this species.

A striking bird associated with forest edges, scrublands, agricultural landscapes and open habitats.

Avian diversity

NON-VENOMOUS

Python molurus

A large non-venomous snake associated with forests, grasslands and habitats close to water.

HabitatForest, wetlands & water edges
