THE SUNDARGARH EXPERIENCE

Where forests meet water, hills and living culture.

Sundergarh offers a rich landscape for low-impact recreation. The division's flagship eco-tourism destination is Sarafgarh Nature Camp, located beside the Sarafgarh Reservoir and surrounded by forest.

For day visitors, the wider Sundergarh landscape also offers waterfalls, picnic landscapes and nearby cultural-natural circuits. This page separates the officially documented Forest Division nature-camp experience from nearby day destinations so visitors can plan responsibly.

Sarafgarh Nature Camp

The documented eco-tourism destination of Sundergarh Forest Division.

Sarafgarh Nature Camp is situated at Badhunjharia on the bank of the Sarafgarh Dam and Reservoir. An official Odisha eco-tourism roadmap identifies Sarafgarh as a nature camp under Sundergarh Forest Division, with 8 rooms documented in the roadmap.

SAMPLE ITINERARY

Sarafgarh Nature Day Circuit

A suggested planning framework — not an official departmental itinerary.

08:00Arrive in the Sarafgarh area and begin a quiet forest / reservoir orientation.
09:30Nature observation, birding and photography around permitted areas.
11:30Visit the nature-camp area and enjoy the landscape; use only approved facilities.
13:00Local/community meal where available and appropriate.
15:00Explore a nearby day destination such as a picnic landscape or waterfall subject to access conditions.
17:00Return before dusk and leave no waste behind.

TRAVEL RESPONSIBLY

Leave only footprints. Take only memories.

Do not litter, feed wildlife, play loud music, enter restricted forest areas or approach animals. During monsoon and after heavy rain, waterfalls, rocks and forest tracks can become hazardous.
