ABOUT • HISTORY • HERITAGE

Sundergarh — Where Forests Tell Stories

A landscape shaped by forests, hills, rivers, wildlife and communities, Sundergarh carries a distinctive natural and cultural identity in north-western Odisha.

As a district, Sundargarh was constituted on 1 January 1948 from the former princely states of Gangpur and Bonai following their merger with Odisha. Its forests, rugged hill ranges, river valleys and rich biodiversity continue to define the character of the region.
